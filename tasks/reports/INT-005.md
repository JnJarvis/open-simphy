## Limits and follow-ups

This is experimental circle/distance mechanics, not full SimPHY solver/API parity.
Collision sounds and event callbacks remain unavailable and conspicuously labeled.
The JS bridge reads initialization/action-staged body state; it is not a general
live simulation scripting API. World.clear means removing runtime additions in
this profile, based on the screenshot's retained authored original. Stored dynamic
joint records are replaced by script reconstruction. Non-circle/compound fixtures,
other joints, circuits, optics, fields/controllers and general scripts need future
explicitly scoped work. Button bounds are adapted to the viewport; read-only source
descriptions dock at top right. The rest of SimPHY's authoring panels/tools are not
implemented by this task. Source brush transforms and exact antialiasing/solver
settings remain approximate. Native body persistence remains separate work.
