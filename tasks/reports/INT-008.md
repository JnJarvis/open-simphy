## Limitations and follow-up proposals

Exact source solver/visual parity remains unestablished. Winding is an independently
implemented physical no-slip bilateral cable constraint, checked against a short
public-API impulse vector; linked spindles and arbitrary extreme-input equivalence
are outside the profile. Planes are finite; procedural brush patterns use source
fill color. Concave or >8-vertex polygons require explicit decomposition, not a
convex hull approximation. Fields/charges/additional body modes, joint families,
controller properties other than friction, scripted widgets/graphs, live event/audio
callbacks, optics/circuits/3D/canvas remain genuine missing behaviors.

Coordinator follow-up proposals: extend common mechanics joints and exact polygon
pieces; implement other controller/property ports and widget/graph bindings; cover
field/charge and remaining mechanics examples; then independently scope optical,
circuit and3D engines/scripts with per-file runtime checks. Integrate approved
INT-007 navigation/dialog changes through normal reviewed merge/conflict handling.
No task is marked DONE until independent review, CI and merge.
