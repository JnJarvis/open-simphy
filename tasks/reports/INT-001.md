## Delivered scope

Changed paths: src/app/{CMakeLists.txt,session.hpp,session.cpp,sdl_host.hpp,
sdl_host.cpp,main.cpp}, tests/integration/app/{CMakeLists.txt,session.cpp}, docs/demo.md.
No producer contracts changed. SDL remains private to the executable; the private
controller uses the accepted scene, physics and renderer modules. Headless tests
compile that controller without acquiring SDL or requiring a display.

The documented three-particle scene starts paused. Space toggles playback, Right
pauses and advances one step, R resets while preserving playback state, Escape or
close exits. The timestep is always 1/128 s. The host accumulates elapsed time,
caps catch-up at 32 steps per iteration, and discards wall time beyond .25 s.
Camera scale is 80 physical pixels/meter, center (0,0). No collisions/editor added.

The SDL adapter owns initialization/window/renderer/texture through RAII in reverse
destruction order. It queries physical output size, disables logical scaling and
texture blending, uses literal RGBA32, copies all rows using the returned pitch,
and recreates the texture on resize. Minimized output skips rendering. Rendering
or presentation failure pauses playback, retains the last successful CPU frame,
and reports diagnostics in the title and console. Fatal errors unwind at main.
